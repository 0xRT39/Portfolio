- Diagnosed usability failures in 5 interfaces through think-aloud tests, cognitive walkthroughs, NASA-TLX, and readability audits, tracing each to a named principle such as pre-attentive pop-out, Gestalt grouping, or working-memory load.
- Slashed **smart-TV input-switching time from 6.7 to 4.4 s and errors from 12% to 5%**, cut **game-launcher frustration from 90 to 20 on NASA-TLX**, and sped **combat-cue reactions from 250 to 200 ms** with 7:1+ contrast outlines.
- Traced 13–20-minute GIMP exports among 3 novices to jargon-heavy dialogs at an 11th–12th grade reading level, and surfaced 3 mental-model friction points in Ableton Live 12 through an 8-step cognitive walkthrough.
