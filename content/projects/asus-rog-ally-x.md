- Crafted a 17-page protocol mapping 5 research questions to 5 peripheral-free tasks, with success, time-on-task, error, and 1–5 difficulty metrics plus scripted moderator rules (15-second silence prompt, 5-minute skip offer).
- Exposed desktop-sized touch targets (**drawing rated 4/5 difficulty, 1/5 satisfaction**), unlabeled gamepad click mappings, and a misfiled brightness control; proposed larger targets, on-screen button hints, and a touch-first settings panel.
- Moderated an in-person think-aloud session with synchronized video, audio, and screen capture, structured as a 30-minute block (5-minute intro, 20 minutes of tasks, 5-minute interview) from a full device reset.
