summary: Arduino wristband that turns rising overstimulation into light and vibration cues for adults with ADHD.

tags: [Product design, UX engineering]

- Benchmarked 7 wearables and 21 studies, then designed 3 feedback states (Focus, Regulation, Recovery) with distinct light and haptic patterns, governed by a 7-rule logic table with fallbacks for unreliable sensor data.
- Engineered an 800+ line Arduino prototype fusing 4 sensors via personal-baseline deltas, 2-of-3 signal voting, and a 2.5-second dwell against flicker; trimmed a 12-part, $120 build to an MVP and moved the band from rigid PLA to flexible TPU.
