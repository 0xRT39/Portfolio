- Designed and coded streaming responses, source citations, confidence cues, editable AI suggestions, and error recovery in Next.js and TypeScript against a REST API, applying human-AI interaction guidelines and explainable AI (XAI) patterns.
- Ran 3 rounds of moderated usability testing (n = 12) and an unmoderated Maze study (n = 60), **raising task success from 58% to 92% and the System Usability Scale (SUS) score from 61 to 88**.
