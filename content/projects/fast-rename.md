- Assessed 3 agencies’ name-change journeys with Morville’s 7-principle UX Honeycomb after pivoting from screen-level heuristics, exposing **a 6–8-month, $230+ process with up to 3-hour phone waits**.
- Specified 30+ requirements (including WCAG 2.0 AA, Section 508, and non-Latin names) and a 4-level prototype plan from paper sketches to a full-scale kiosk, with sign-off gates for 5 stakeholder groups and a 50% time-reduction target.
- Conceived a Staples-partnered, 3-tier concierge service ($299–$849) with a persona per tier, an 8-stage future-state experience map, and a storyboard, promising a 7-day passport turnaround in the mid tier.
