- Rubric-scored 500 interfaces generated by 4 Large Language Models (LLMs) on 6 dimensions (user flows, layout, visual hierarchy, typography, usability, WCAG 2.2 AA), with pairwise preference ranking and written design rationale for each failure.
- Trained 5 raters to an inter-rater reliability of 0.82 (Cohen’s kappa) and built a 22-category failure taxonomy showing that **64% of visually polished outputs hid accessibility or hierarchy defects**.
