- Combined a Qualtrics survey (n = 240), a 2-week diary study, and 12 user interviews into Jobs to Be Done (JTBD), 3 personas, and a journey map that pinpointed identity verification as the main drop-off.
- Redesigned the onboarding flow mobile-first in Figma with a high-fidelity prototype and UX writing pass; an A/B test tracked in Google Analytics 4 (GA4) **lifted completion from 46% to 71%**.
