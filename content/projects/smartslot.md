- Uncovered **a 33% scheduler task-failure rate and a 6x spread in completion time (31 vs. 185 s)** through think-aloud tests of 9 tasks across 3 role-based workflows, then severity-rated 14 issues by failure, efficiency, trust, and confusion.
- Delivered 12 before-and-after fixes, including an AI-suggestion pop-up, urgency-ordered patient queues, and color-coded availability, and built in staff sign-off on AI suggestions, same-day care guarantees, and monthly bias audits.
- Grounded the design in 27 peer-reviewed sources showing single interventions lift adherence only 3–14%, justifying a combined system of no-show-aware double-booking, hospital-provided rides, and patient self-rescheduling.
