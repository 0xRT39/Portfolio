- Moderated 5 remote think-aloud sessions on UserTesting and coded them in a rainbow spreadsheet and affinity map, producing 6 findings (3 strengths, 3 problems) backed by participant counts, quotes, and video highlight clips.
- Pinpointed a sign-up path hidden behind “Sign In” that **confused 4 of 5 users** and front-loaded instructions that **overwhelmed 3 of 5**; prioritized fixes including a dedicated Sign Up button, a free first lesson, and bite-sized hints.
- Scripted the screener, test plan, and 15-minute think-aloud protocol for US adults new to the app, tightening the script after a pilot session overran its slot.
