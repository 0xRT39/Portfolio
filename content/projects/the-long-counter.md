- Logged 7+ hours of fieldwork across 3 site visits, 5 interviews, a comparison store, and 20 years of online discussion on 8 platforms, mapping an 8-step customer behavior pattern held in place by unwritten norms.
- Derived 4 design implications from 15 sources in a 4,600-word report, including that formalizing norms trades one kind of newcomer friction for another and that long-relationship businesses need metrics beyond conversion.
