- Co-designed with 12 wheelchair users; built switch-, voice-, and touch-operable controls exceeding WCAG 2.1 AA (7:1 contrast, 44 px targets), **lowering task time by 38%**.
- Validated through VoiceOver and switch-control usability testing, **raising task success from 64% to 96%**.
